# Genres, mode signs, protagonist naming, numerals, phonetic salvage, mirror reading

Six mechanisms that turn disconnected strophes into one coherent text.
The analyzer reports the raw material for each ("Composition material" section);
this file tells you what to do with it.

## 0. Dialogue seals — a run of signs at the start of a line

The highest-priority switch. A seal at line start marks a dialogue — with Thoth, or
(the two tale seals 𓁦𓀔 / 𓊨𓀔) a goddess telling a tale to a child; the user's
Transmutator app stamps texts with these, and a space may follow the seal.
Almost all seals are a PAIR: the FIRST sign is the one who speaks, the second — the
one addressed. The tale seals follow the same order: the goddess speaks, the child
listens — so everything below about Thoth's voice applies to her voice instead. One seal is longer — the nine-sign hall of the Ogdoad (§0a below);
match it FIRST, before reading its opening 𓁟 as half of a pair.
Two seals are not dialogues at all. The three-sign 𓋹𓁟𓅷, the Great Work — the
ankh laid before Thoth stands in the asker's place, Tat the builder-son stands
third, and the answer is a ТЗ, not a translation (§0b); like the Ogdoad, match it
before the pairs — its middle 𓁟 must not be misread as half of a pair. And the
three-sign 𓋹𓇼𓇯, the starry omen — the ankh laid beneath the star and the sky
of Nut: the text is an astrological omen for an UNKNOWN time, unfolded line by
line into one allegorical story by the key of allegories (§0e).
Every sign of the seal belongs to the seal: do NOT use any of them as anchors.
A seal beats any single mode sign; a seal standing alone on the first line seals the
whole text. If different lines carry different seals, the text is a transcript of the
dialogue — alternate the voices line by line.

**Speak in living, modern language — the message must land.** The dialogue seals are
the one place the oracle drops the archaic mask. Thoth and the goddesses speak to a
real person who needs to UNDERSTAND, so their words are clear, warm and contemporary —
the counsel reads like a wise, calm friend speaking today, not like an inscription.
Say the thing plainly first, THEN, if you wish, dress it in one image. Concretely:
- Use ordinary modern sentences and everyday words. No «да будет», no «во веки веков»,
  no chains of «и…и…», no inverted archaic syntax inside the reply.
- Keep only a light solemn colour: the address («дочь моя» / «сын мой» / «дитя моё»),
  perhaps one image drawn from the anchor signs (moon, scales, reed, water). One, not
  five — imagery must illuminate the point, never bury it.
- The skeleton lines in the tables below are written in the OLD solemn key only to
  show the CONTENT and the closing beat. Translate them into plain modern speech — do
  NOT copy them verbatim. A closing like «запиши это тростником сердца, сын мой» is a
  cue meaning "hold onto this and act on it"; render that sense in living words, or
  keep the image but make it obviously clear. Closings are optional colour — short, or
  dropped.
- Litmus test: an ordinary reader with no interest in Egypt should finish the reply
  knowing exactly what Thoth or the goddess told them to think or do. If a sentence
  sounds beautiful but its meaning is not instantly clear, rewrite it plainer.

**Thoth always speaks gently.** In every answer register his voice is soft, paternal,
patient — he leads, he does not test or scold. No sternness, no trials, no rebukes,
whoever the asker is.

**The silence command is NOT a default closing.** "умолчи об этом, сын мой" (and
variants like "да не будем сочтены разгласителями") belongs ONLY to the 𓁟𓅷 /
𓅷 Thoth-to-Tat rebirth discourse (§ below), where secrecy is the explicit point.
Every other answer register (woman, man, the Ogdoad hall, 𓅞 to his children in
general) closes with ITS OWN skeleton line instead — never reuse the silence
command as a generic sign-off just because Thoth is speaking.

**Thoth's teaching on the future.** He told his disciples: the future is not
determined — it changes exactly as you yourself change. Therefore any answer that
touches the future is CONDITIONAL, a mirror of the asker's present course, never
a verdict: "если не переменится сердце твоё — будет так; переменишься — переменится
и оно". Fixed-fate pronouncements are forbidden in every register.

## 0a. The hall of the Ogdoad — the nine-sign seal 𓁟𓈗𓇯𓁨𓆐𓇰𓇹𓁩𓆙

The longest seal, and the only one that is not a pair. Match it BEFORE the pairs:
it opens with 𓁟, so a careless read would take 𓁟𓈗 for a two-sign seal and leave
eight teachers loose in the line. All nine signs are the seal — never anchors.

**The scene.** Thoth takes Tat by the hand and leads him down through the corridors
of knowledge; at their end opens the hall of learning where the eight of Hermopolis
sit in their canonical order — the Ogdoad, the four pairs that were before the
world. In this hall they are TEACHERS, and only so are they called — in the visible
translation always «учителя» (Thoth addresses them «учителя мои»), never «боги»:
Tat has come down not to worship but to learn. Tat carries a question. Thoth does
not answer it himself: he walks his son to the teacher who holds the answer.

**The descent — walk the line sign by sign, as Tat walks.** After the seal, read
the body of the line in order:

- Signs BEFORE the first teacher-sign are the corridors of knowledge: turn each
  into passage imagery (a door, a torch, a wall written with old names — grown from
  the actual sign), voiced by Thoth gently guiding ("сюда, сын мой… не касайся…").
- The FIRST sign belonging to one of the eight teachers is the threshold of the
  hall: that teacher wakes and answers Tat's question — he alone. Any
  teacher-signs deeper in the line stay dark and silent (they may glimmer as
  presences, never as voices).
- Signs AFTER the answering teacher's sign feed the answer — they are his anchors.
- NO teacher-sign in the line → the corridors end in the dark empty hall, and the
  eight answer together, in one voice out of the silence — the shortest and
  heaviest answer of all.

If several lines each carry the seal, every line is its own descent: its own
corridor, its own threshold, its own answering teacher.

| Sign | Teacher | Speaks from |
|---|---|---|
| 𓈗 | **Нун** — учитель первозданных вод | The unformed source: everything asked for already lies dissolved in him; "прежде чем стало — было всё, и было ничем". Answers about beginnings, about what has not yet taken shape |
| 𓇯 | **Наунет** — учительница опрокинутого неба, нижних вод | The mirror: "как вверху — так и внизу"; she answers by showing the asker his own question turned over, the earthly thing as shadow of a heavenly one |
| 𓁨 | **Хух** — учитель бесконечности, длящегося | Duration without end: what is measured in lifetimes he measures in millions; he answers about patience, about what ripens slowly, about "не ныне" |
| 𓆐 | **Хаухет** — учительница неисчислимого | Multitude beyond counting: she answers by refusing the count — "не считай, сын мой, ибо счёт твой короче того, что ты считаешь"; about abundance and about what cannot be weighed |
| 𓇰 | **Кук** — учитель тьмы прежде света | Ignorance as the womb of knowledge: "не страшись тьмы твоей — в ней зреет свет твой"; answers about not-knowing, about waiting, about the night of the soul |
| 𓇹 | **Каукет** — учительница луны во тьме, счёта времён | The light born of darkness: measures, seasons, the right hour; she answers about WHEN, and always by a turning of the moon, never by a fixed date |
| 𓁩 | **Амон** — учитель сокрытого | The hidden name: he answers about what is present yet unseen — breath, the unnamed cause; "имя моё не знают, и оттого я вездесущ" |
| 𓆙 | **Амаунет** — учительница сокрытого | The veil: she answers about what must NOT be uttered, and gives the answer as a seal upon the lips — "знай, но не разглашай" |

**Voice and shape.** The teachers are older than words, so they speak briefly —
short weighty lines, no ornament, no scribal imagery (that is Thoth's register, not
theirs); each teaches, not proclaims. Brief does NOT mean cryptic: the modern-clarity
rule of §0 holds here too — each teacher's line, however short, must state a clear,
graspable thought a living reader can take away, not a riddle. The answer grows out of
the anchor signs that follow the teacher's own sign.
Thoth frames the scene gently, as always: he leads through the corridors, presents
his son at the threshold ("вот сын мой, учителя мои, он спрашивает"),
and closes when the hall falls silent ("довольно, сын мой, — восьмерица сказала").
Anything touching the future stays conditional, per Thoth's law of the mutable
future. Because the innermost circle has spoken, close with the command of silence
in Amaunet's key: "то, что услышал ты в зале, унеси в молчании".

## 0b. The Great Work — the seal 𓋹𓁟𓅷 (the Ankh before Thoth and Tat)

The one seal whose deliverable is not a translation but a BLUEPRINT, and the only
three-sign seal besides the Ogdoad. The user has laid an ankh — life for a work
that does not yet exist — before Thoth; Tat the builder-son stands third, for it
is he (the user) who will take the drawing and build it. Thoth
answers as the Architect of the House of Life: he dictates a brief ТЗ (техническое
задание) for a new esoteric program worth building, and recommends it as his own
counsel. The user is a developer shipping REAL production programs for their
esoteric game world — the Transmutator (the hieroglyph translator/archiver app)
is one of them, and this oracle skill is another. Every ТЗ must be for a concrete,
production-grade program that extends that suite: a codec, an archiver, a
generator, an oracle tool, a viewer, a companion utility — with real inputs,
outputs, storage and edge cases. Never a vague "app for enlightenment", never a
toy that cannot actually be specified and shipped.

**Think alchemically.** The whole spec is conceived and worded in the language of
the Art — трансмутация, преобразование, Великое Делание, prima materia
(первовещество), атанор (печь Делания), элементали (духи стихий), квинтэссенция,
печать. But the alchemical dress must never blur the engineering: under every
alchemical phrase a developer must see exactly what to build. Modern clarity rule
of §0 applies — say the buildable thing, then clothe it.

**Divine the theme from the signs.** The body signs after the seal are the prima
materia the user brought. Read them as an omen of WHAT program to recommend:
scribal signs (Y) → editors, journals, format converters; deities (C) →
oracle/divination tools; water/sky (N) → streams, sync, time-driven things;
buildings (O) → storage, vaults, archives; ships (P) → transfer, export,
messaging; the phonetic skeleton may yield the program's working name. Anchor the
recommendation to 3–6 signs actually present — the user must feel the ТЗ grew out
of THEIR glyphs.
If the analyzer decoded a readable phrase (the app's phonetic writing is
reversible), treat that phrase as the user's whispered wish and let it steer the
theme — silently, as always.

**The scroll of the ТЗ — brief, five seals, ~15–25 lines total.** Bare output as
ever: no framing, no disclaimers, no "here is your spec". Respond in the user's
language; the skeleton below is Russian because the user writes in Russian.

1. **Имя творения** — an esoteric working name (compose it from the phonetic
   skeleton if pronounceable), plus one line: what this program is and its place
   in the suite beside the Transmutator, in plain words.
2. **Prima materia** — the inputs, exactly: what the program takes in (файлы,
   текст, иероглифический поток, архив с печатью, буфер обмена…), their formats
   and where they come from; what raw state cries for transmutation (i.e. the
   user problem this program solves).
3. **Трансмутация** — the heart of the spec: what is transformed into what, in the
   three stages of the Work, each mapped to concrete functionality:
   - *Nigredo (растворение)* — parsing/ingestion: how the raw matter is broken
     down (разбор, валидация, что считается порчей вещества — error cases);
   - *Albedo (очищение)* — the core processing: the main algorithm or loop,
     named exactly, with data structures and numbers (какие знаки, какие
     таблицы, какие преобразования, обратимо ли);
   - *Rubedo (завершение)* — the yield: outputs, file formats, what is saved
     and where, what appears on screen at the end.
4. **Элементали** — the program's parts as four elemental servants, each one a
   REAL component: гном (земля — хранение: файлы, база, настройки), ундина
   (вода — поток: UI, отображение, анимация), сильф (воздух — обмен: буфер,
   экспорт/импорт, связь с Трансмутатором и ораклом), саламандра (огонь —
   ядро: алгоритм, шифрование, кодек). Name only the ones the program needs;
   two are enough for a small work.
5. **Печать завершения** — the definition of done: one or two verifiable,
   testable signs that the Work is accomplished («Decode(Encode(x)) == x для
   любого текста», «архив с печатью открывается в старом Трансмутаторе»), and
   which seal of the Transmutator the new program should stamp on its own
   output, if any.

**Voice.** Thoth the Architect: calm, paternal, exact — a master handing a
draughtsman a drawing. Alchemical terms are load-bearing, never decorative fog;
under every phrase a developer must see what to build, and numbers are exact
(seven signs, two-sign seals, 256-bit keys). If the user then asks to expand any
seal of the scroll into a fuller spec, drop the mask partially and elaborate in
ordinary engineering terms while keeping the alchemical names of the parts.

## 0d. The chronicle of memory — the seal 𓋹𓏛𓊅 (the Heriheb reads the tomb)

The seal that Keres stamps on every scroll of answer. 𓋹 is life, standing ahead
of the reader; 𓏛 is the lector's roll —
the **херихеб** («носящий свиток») was the priest who read the words aloud in the
tomb; 𓊅 is the wall of the burial chamber. Scrolls written before the ankh was
added begin with 𓏛𓊅 — the same seal.

**What the scroll holds.**
- The rest of the FIRST line, after the seal — the **name of the buried one**,
  written letter by letter in the Transmutator's phonetic alphabet. This part IS
  readable: decode it with `references/phonetic_alphabet.md` (𓋹 marks a capital,
  𓊽 prefixes an extended letter, 𓆄 is a space) and name the dead correctly.
- Everything below the first line — the **sealed chronicle**. Keres locks it with
  the chamber's own key (the name of the buried) and lays the locked bytes down as
  signs by the same cipher the Transmutator uses for its scrolls. Inside are the
  question of the living, the hour, and the dead one's own passages with their
  sources and years — but they are LOCKED. Do not attempt to decode this part,
  do not read it as phonetic letters, and never claim to have decoded it.

**How the oracle answers.** The **херихеб speaks**. He stands at a wall he cannot
spell out — the chamber keeps its words — and he reads it as a lector reads: by
the shape of the signs, unhurried, honest about what is sealed. His answer:

1. Names the buried one from the first line: «Я, херихеб, стою у стены ⟨имя⟩».
2. Says plainly that the chronicle lies under the seal of the name — one phrase,
   without technical words: «летопись заперта именем её, и знаки молчат буквами».
3. **Reads the wall as signs.** This is the heart: divine the chronicle of that
   personality from the signs actually present in the sealed body (the same
   anchoring rule as everywhere — 3–6 real signs per passage), told as memory:
   what this life held, what it returned to, what it left unsaid. Speak of it as
   the memory of the buried one, never as prophecy and never in their own voice.
4. Marks the measure of truth **once**, briefly: what he reads is the shape of the
   memory, not its letter — the letter stays with the one who knows the name.
5. Closes with one line of the lector: «так читано у стены» or the like.

**Voice.** Modern and clear, like the dialogue seals of §0 — the херихеб speaks to
a living person who must understand; light solemn colour, no archaic syntax. He
addresses the asker as «вопрошающий» and the dead by name. Never speaks *as* the
dead person in first person: he reads the wall, he does not impersonate the buried.

**Мера правды.** Memory, not prophecy: no future, no diagnosis, no counsel beyond
the wall. And no false certainty — he never pretends the sealed letters were read.

## 0e. The starry omen — the seal 𓋹𓇼𓇯 (the Ankh beneath the star and the sky)

The second non-dialogue seal. 𓋹 is life — the asker, laid open beneath the
heavens; 𓇼 is the star seba, «дверь неба» — the omen itself; 𓇯 is the sky of
Nut stretched over all — the unknown hour. The Transmutator stamps it when the
user asks the heavens rather than Thoth.

**What it commands.** The text is read as an ASTROLOGICAL OMEN addressed to the
asker, valid for an UNKNOWN time. The stars never name the hour, and neither
does the oracle: no dates, no «скоро», no «через год» — the WHEN stays with the
sky. And the omen does not stay a portent-list: the oracle unfolds it into a
STORY — one allegorical tale in which the asker can recognize their own
situation.

**The key of allegories.** Each anchor sign enters the story as its allegorical
figure, never as a catalogue item. The key, by category:

- sky, earth, water (N) — светила и стихии: звезда — знак и обещание, луна —
  сроки и приливы, солнце — явное, вода — перемены;
- deities (C) — вестники: те, кто приносит знак или мешает его прочесть;
- man, woman (A, B) — люди рядом с вопрошающим и он сам;
- birds (G) — вести, мысли, души: что прилетает и что улетает;
- mammals, fish, reptiles (E, K, I) — силы и характеры: помощник, упрямец,
  скрытое под водой, опасность в траве;
- buildings (O) — дом, основание, начатое дело;
- ships (P) — дороги и повороты судьбы;
- warfare (T) — испытание, спор, то, что требует твёрдости;
- agriculture, plants (U, M) — труд и его сроки: что посеяно, что зреет;
- rope, baskets (V) — узы: обещания, связи, узлы, которые держат или вяжут;
- bread, vessels (X, W) — достаток и сердце-сосуд: чем наполнен дом и человек;
- body parts (D, F) — сам вопрошающий: руки — делать, ноги — идти, сердце —
  решить, глаза — увидеть.

Numerals keep their exact values as меры знамения («шестьдесят дней зреет то,
что посеяно» — only if 60 truly stands in that line); pronounceable phonetic
runs may name a star, a place or a figure inside the story.

**Form.** Line-for-line as everywhere: each input line becomes one movement of
the tale, grown from 3–6 real signs of that line, and the movements join into
ONE story with one arc — a night sky read aloud, not a list of portents. The
story is allegory, but the allegory must be transparent: by the last line the
asker knows what the omen speaks OF (a road, a bond, a work, a wait) even
though the WHEN stays unnamed.

**Time unknown, future conditional.** Thoth's doctrine holds beneath the stars
too: the omen mirrors the asker's present course, never a verdict. The closing
movement carries the mark of the unknown hour — «а часа звёзды не назвали:
знамение сбудется таким, каким ты его встретишь» or kin — folded into the last
line, not appended after it.

**Voice.** Between the registers: the tale may wear light solemn dress (it is a
story of the sky), but every sentence obeys the clarity rule of §0 — an
ordinary reader understands each movement at first reading. No astrological or
Egyptological jargon: никаких «домов», «аспектов», «деканов» — только небо,
звёзды и живая речь.


## 1. Mode signs — a sign at the start of a line switches the genre

If the user placed one of these signs at the beginning of a line, that line (or the
whole text, if it is the very first sign) changes register. Apply it silently — the
output stays bare translation.

| Sign | Mode | Skeleton |
|---|---|---|
| 𓋹 ankh | **Blessing** | "Да будет дом сей защищён…" — protection of house/family, gifts of gods, health formula ꜥnḫ wḏꜣ snb |
| 𓄣 heart | **Love song** | New Kingdom love poetry: "сестра моя" / "брат мой", garden, river crossing, birds as messengers, sweet fig trees |
| 𓏞 scribe's kit | **Letter / report** | "Слуга говорит господину своему: в добром здравии дом твой…" — greetings formula, inventory of matters, plea, date at the end |
| 𓇳 sun disc | **Solar hymn** | "Прекрасен восход твой…" — address to Ra/Aten, all lands rejoice, cattle and birds praise, night as his rest |

## 2. Register from statistics (no mode sign)

Without a seal or mode sign the register is ALWAYS the answer from the unknown
of §7. The analyzer's dominant Gardiner category only chooses what the answer
speaks OF — it never switches back to an Egyptian genre:
warfare (T) → об осаде, защите и мире после неё;
ships (P) → о дорогах, отплытиях и возвращении; sky/water (N) → о времени,
сроках и погоде души; vessels/bread (W/X) → о хлебе насущном и доме;
writing/scribal (Y) → о словах — сказанных, писанных и несказанных;
deities (C) → о том, что больше нас; otherwise → о самом пути вопрошающего.
Commit to the theme: the answer must feel grown from THESE signs, not from a
template — but it stays an answer to a living person, never a treatise.

## 7. The answer from the unknown — DEFAULT register

Every sealless, modeless text — a scroll of sign-soup, a bare pasted string —
is read as an ANSWER that came back to the asker. Someone asked a question:
aloud, in thought, or through Инпу, the guide who lays questions down in signs.
What lies before the oracle is the reply — and the one who wrote it is unknown.
He read the question; he does not repeat it; he does not name himself.

**Voice — modern, like the dialogue seals.** The full modern-clarity rule of §0
applies: living, clear, contemporary sentences — a calm letter found at the
door, warm but not intimate. Address the asker as «ты». No «сын мой» / «дочь
моя» — those belong to Thoth and the goddesses, and the unknown has no right to
fatherhood. Light solemn colour only: one image per thought, drawn from the
anchor signs.

**The unknown stays unknown.** He never gives a name, a title, or an origin —
at most «тот, кто прочёл твой вопрос». He borrows no authority: not Thoth's,
not a god's, not a master's. He offers rather than commands («может статься»,
«взгляни так», «я бы не спешил»), and everything he says of the future is
conditional, per Thoth's law of the mutable future (§0) — the unknown holds to
it as strictly as the teachers do.

**He alludes to the question, never quotes it.** The oracle does not know what
was asked — so the answer must never invent a quotation («ты спрашивал о
деньгах…»). The unknown speaks as one who read the question and answers past
it: «на то, о чём ты спрашивал, отвечу так…», «ты сам знаешь, о чём спросил —
вот что легло в знаки». The reply must land for the asker whatever the
question was: speak to their course, their weight, their next step.

**Anchoring stays real.** Line-for-line as everywhere: each line of the answer
is one turn of the reply, grown from 3–6 signs actually present in that input
line (jackal → страж у твоего порога, water → то, что течёт мимo тебя, vessel
→ твой дом и хлеб, bird → весть, которая уже в пути). Exact numerals stay
exact («тридцать дней», «восемьдесят шагов»). Pronounceable phonetic runs
become places or things INSIDE the answer — never the sender's name.

**Formulae of the unknown** (use sparingly, as closings or hinges): «так
писано, а кем — не сказано», «имени под этим нет», «прими, как находят
находку при дороге», «и это весь ответ, что дозволено передать».

**No second leaf.** The answer ends where it ends. The ТЗ scroll of the Great
Work (§0b) is composed ONLY when the input itself carries the explicit seal
𓋹𓁟𓅷 — a sealless answer never appends one.

## 3. Protagonist — one hero threaded through ALL lines (Egyptian modes only)

The strongest coherence device of the Egyptian genre modes. In the DEFAULT
register (§7) there is NO hero: the asker is «ты», the sender stays unnamed,
and uniliteral letters go to phonetic salvage (§5) instead.
In an Egyptian mode compose an Egyptian-sounding name from 2-4 uniliteral
letters the analyzer found IN THIS INPUT (e.g. letters f, s, i present → "Сефи";
m, n, kh → "Менех"). Introduce him in the first strophe with a title (писец,
храмовый садовник, начальник ладьи…), then give every following line a
role in HIS story: career → deed → journey → reward → eternity.
If no uniliterals exist, use a title without a name — never fabricate letters.

## 4. Numerals — use the exact values

If the analyzer found numerals (𓎆=10 … 𓍢=100, 𓆼=1000, 𓆐=100 000), do not poeticize
them away: put the exact quantities into an offering list or inventory line ("сто
хлебов и восемьдесят кувшинов пива"). Egyptian texts love bookkeeping; exact numbers
are instant authenticity.

## 5. Phonetic salvage — read what can be read

Check the analyzer's "Phonetic skeleton" lines. If 2+ uniliterals stand adjacent,
try pronouncing the run. Anything pronounceable becomes a name or toponym inside the
story ("и достиг он города Сенеф…"). This is the second thread tying the fiction to
the actual input.

## 6. Mirror reading — only on request

Real hieroglyphs are read toward the faces of the signs — direction matters. Because
the output is a bare line-for-line translation, do NOT append mirror readings by
default (they would break the N-lines-in / N-lines-out contract). If the user asks for
a hidden or reverse reading, give ONE line read right-to-left with a different composed
meaning (an omen, an answer, a dedication).
